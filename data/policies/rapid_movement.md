# AML-POL-03: Rapid Movement of Funds

## Section 3.1 - Typology
Rapid movement (pass-through activity) means funds arrive in an account and leave again within a very short time, with little business purpose. It is often used to layer funds between accounts.

## Section 3.2 - Red-flag indicators
- 80% or more of an incoming transfer is sent out again within 48 hours.
- Combined incoming transfers in 7 days above 3x the expected monthly turnover.
- Accounts under 12 months old with turnover far above the declared profile.
- Outgoing payments to newly seen counterparties with vague narrations.

## Section 3.3 - Mitigating factors and analyst actions
Documented recurring payments such as payroll or property leases, paid on a stable schedule to stable counterparties in amounts consistent with account history, are normally explained. Check history, counterparty and narration, and screen new counterparties.

## Section 3.4 - Escalation and confidentiality
Escalate when the indicators in 3.2 are met without a credible explanation. Analysts recommend only; a human officer closes alerts. Never tip off the customer or tell the customer about the investigation.
