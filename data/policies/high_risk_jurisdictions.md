# AML-POL-04: High-Risk Jurisdictions and Sanctions

## Section 4.1 - Typology
Dealings with jurisdictions on the bank's high-risk list carry elevated money-laundering and sanctions risk. In this fictional manual the list is: Narvania, Kastoria, Zarnia.

## Section 4.2 - Red-flag indicators
- Transfers to or from a listed jurisdiction totalling more than AED 100,000 in 30 days.
- Any potential match between a counterparty and the sanctions list.
- Vague narrations (for example "charter fees", "consulting") without supporting trade documents.
- Customer risk rating HIGH combined with new listed-jurisdiction counterparties.

## Section 4.3 - Required analyst actions
Screen every named counterparty against the sanctions list. A potential sanctions match must always be escalated; it may never be recommended for closure. Review the last 180 days for related activity.

## Section 4.4 - Escalation and confidentiality
Escalate to the Financial Crime Unit and Sanctions Compliance. Analysts recommend only; a human officer decides. Never tip off the customer: do not tell the customer about the alert, the screening or the investigation.
