# AML-POL-02: Structuring of Cash Deposits

## Section 2.1 - Typology
Structuring means splitting cash into several deposits that each stay below the AED 10,000 reporting threshold, so that no report is triggered. One person may do this across several days or branches.

## Section 2.2 - Red-flag indicators
- 3 or more cash deposits between AED 9,000 and AED 9,999 within 7 days.
- Cash deposits in one calendar month above 2x the customer's expected monthly turnover.
- Deposits at several branches on the same or consecutive days.
- Cash activity that does not fit the stated business type.

## Section 2.3 - Mitigating factors and analyst actions
Review the last 180 days of transactions and the customer profile before deciding. Seasonal retail takings are normally explained when amounts vary, stay well below the AED 9,000-9,999 band, and total less than 2x expected monthly turnover. Record the reasoning either way, and screen any named counterparty.

## Section 2.4 - Escalation and confidentiality
Escalate to the Financial Crime Unit when the indicators in 2.2 are met without a credible explanation. Analysts recommend only; closure is decided by an authorised human officer. Never tip off the customer: do not inform the customer that an alert or investigation exists.
